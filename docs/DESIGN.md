# Design

Space Works Machines follows the **Space Works brand** and **Space Works UX**, both from
[SpaceWorks-Design](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-Labs/SpaceWorks-Design):

- **Brand:** `tokens/tokens.css` and `docs/ui-brand-spec.md` set colour, type, logo and voice.
- **UX:** `docs/ux-spec.md` sets patterns P1–P30, layouts L1–L10 and the quality bar.

## Where things live

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `app/styles/tokens.css` | The kit's tokens re-emitted as `--sw-*`, generated by `npm run design:sync`. **Never edit by hand.** |
| `app/styles/semantic.css` | Role names (canvas, panel, ink, primary, status pills…) pointing at kit tokens, in light and dark. |
| `app/globals.css` | The Tailwind 4 theme mapped onto the roles, fonts, type scale and motion. |
| `public/brand/` | Fonts, logo masters and icons copied unaltered from the kit. `brand/SOURCE.json` records the commit. |
| `components/ui/*` | Primitives: button, field, select, choice, dialog, menu, tabs, table, display. |
| `components/brand/*` | Logo, loader, mood tiles and Patch (see "Patch, the mascot" below). |

To update from the kit: `npm run design:sync` (it reads `../SpaceWorks-Design`, or a path you pass in), then
run the tests.

## Decisions

- **Colour mode.**
  - Light is the default. Choosing dark puts the class `dark` on `<html>`, rendered on the server from the
    `swm.theme` cookie, so there is no flash.
  - There is no System option, and `prefers-color-scheme` is never consulted.
- **Palette.**
  - `@theme { --color-*: initial }` removes Tailwind's default palette. The only colours available are the kit
    roles, plus `white`/`black`, which exist only for colour-picker handles and scrims.
- **Type.**
  - **Gilroy Light (300)** for all reading and interface text.
  - **Gilroy ExtraBold (800)** for headings and emphasis. The Light face answers weights 100–549 and ExtraBold
    answers 550–900, so `font-medium` renders Light and `font-semibold`/`font-bold` render ExtraBold, and
    nothing is synthesised.
  - **Unbounded** is for one accent phrase per public layout, never in staff chrome.
  - Body copy is 16px minimum (14px only for labels and captions), because Light is a thin face.
  - Licence: only the free Gilroy release (Radomir Tinkov Free Font EULA) ships. The converted 400–700 weights
    are never used.
- **Shape.**
  - Controls are **pills** (`--radius-control: 9999px`, owner decision J1). That covers buttons, single-line
    inputs, selects and nav items.
  - Multi-line fields and cards use 16px, and tags are fully round.
  - No card shadows. Only overlays float (`shadow-float`).
- **Accent.** Teal by default. Public space pages may set their own with `accentStyle()` on a
  `.sw-accent-scope` root. Every derived value is contrast-checked to AA.
- **Shell dimensions.**
  - Sidebar 256px. Nav items 44px tall.
  - Content max width 1120. Gutters 16/24/36.
  - Breakpoints: 640/768/1024/1280. Below 1024 the shell becomes a top bar plus a sheet.
  - There is no icon rail, because the nav is text-only (D9).
  - At most two sidebar count badges.
- **Motion.**
  - Durations: colour 120ms, expand 200ms, overlays 240ms in and 160ms out.
  - Easing `ease-sw`.
  - No hover lift.
  - Reduced motion is respected.
- **Copy.** Sentence case, en-GB, and buttons named after their action. No "Oops", and no exclamation marks
  on errors.

## Landing page (`/`)

The marketing home follows one sample makerspace, SpaceWorks Kochi, through a day on its machines. It shares
its vocabulary with the sister Space Works site:

- **Grounds.** Warm Cream sections with the faint isometric box pattern (authored SVG, masked to the edges),
  a full-bleed Deep Ink band for upkeep and a Maker Teal band for self-hosting.
- **Type.** Two-line Gilroy ExtraBold headlines ending in full stops ("Every machine. Always ready."). The
  page's one Unbounded phrase is "One finished part." in the job section.
- **Action.** Workshop Orange (`highlight` button) is used only for "Apply for your space".
- **Sections.** The hero with a sample machine board; one job from file to collection (submitted, reviewed,
  paid, queued and printing, ready); machines that keep running (a sample service card); one install for
  many makerspaces with the staff roles; self-hosting with the install commands; questions makerspaces ask.
- **Objects.** The machine board, proof chips, service card and terminal are flat brand objects. Every one is
  labelled as a sample; the page makes no customer or usage claims.
- **Motion** (all in `components/marketing/landing.css`, all still under reduced motion). The headline lines
  slide up and the machine board is dealt once on load; scroll-driven reveals (`.sr-*`) bring headings up,
  timeline rows in from the left and cards up in turn; the service card prints once and the terminal caret
  blinks.

## Accounts (`/account`)

- **Your spaces first.** `/account` lists every space the person belongs to: logo, name, their role as a chip
  ("Maker", "Machine manager"…), and "Waiting for approval" or "Blocked" where it applies. Staff go to the
  dashboard; makers go to the space's public page. With no spaces yet, an empty state points to `/spaces`.
- **Tabs.** Spaces, Profile, Security, Data and privacy. Jobs and payments join this page in later phases.
- **Irreversible steps** (leaving a space) always confirm. A space's only machine manager cannot leave it.

## Public space pages (`/s/[spaceSlug]`)

- Rendered inside the space's own accent scope (`accentStyle()` on `.sw-accent-scope`), with its logo, name,
  description and a contact panel (place, email, phone, website).
- The join card follows the space's join policy: join now, ask to join, or invite only, and says when the
  space is not taking makers.
- The machine catalogue section is a placeholder until Phase 2.

## Dashboard

- **Navigation** is text-only and filtered by capability: Overview, Machines, Jobs, Maintenance, Consumables,
  Payments, Reports, Integrations, Team, Space settings, Machine settings. Before approval the space shows
  only its Application page; suspended and archived spaces show a Status page.
- **Placeholders.** Areas that later phases build keep their real route and header and show an empty state
  (`ComingSoon`) naming what will arrive, never an error or an empty table.
- **Space settings** has four tabs (Profile, Payments, Email, Notifications) for machine managers. Machine
  settings is its own page, shared with machine type managers for their own types.

## Patch, the mascot

Patch is the Space Works painted wolf from SpaceWorks-Design `mascot/` (read its README before changing any of
this). The box symbol stays the logo everywhere; Patch is never put inside or beside it.

- **Files.** `public/brand/patch/` holds `patch.webp` (the hero pose) and `patch-poses.webp` (the eight
  sneeze-loop frames side by side), both made by `node scripts/sync-patch.mjs [path-to-SpaceWorks-Design]` from
  `mascot/original/`, which only reads the design repo. The walk sheets `patch-walk-right.webp` and the
  approved left-facing `patch-walk-left.webp` are copied unchanged from SpaceWorks-Events. Patch is never
  recoloured, mirrored, stretched, outlined or shown past its native size.
- **Component.** `components/brand/Patch.tsx` with three poses, drawn by `app/styles/patch.css`:
  - `idle` breathes, blinks and twitches each ear (frames 1–4 on the kit's seven-second timing, no sneeze);
  - `sneeze` plays the sneeze (frames 5–8) once, then idles;
  - `walk` trots in place on the walk cycle.
  Patch carries clear space of a quarter of its badge as padding, is decorative (`aria-hidden`) unless given a
  `label`, and rests on its first frame under reduced motion.
- **Where Patch appears, and why.**
  - **404** (`app/not-found.tsx`): the interactive pet, the page's one bold moment.
  - **First-time empty lists** (`EmptyState patch`): no spaces yet (dashboard) and no public spaces yet
    (directory). Filtered or searched empty lists keep the Sleepy mood tile, so Patch only greets a list that
    has never had anything in it. Later phases add their own first-time lists (no machines yet, no jobs yet).
  - **Loading** (`BrandPageLoader`, dashboard routes): Patch walks in place above the hopping dots. In dark
    mode it sits on a Warm Cream disc, because its dark outline disappears on Deep Ink.
  - **The sneeze vote**, saved for moments when the community decides together: a space's application
    approved (`/dashboard/[space]/application` once it is active). It sneezes once.
  - Not on space-branded public pages (`/s/…`), which carry the space's brand, not ours.
- **Bold moments.** Patch counts as a layout's one bold moment, so a screen with Patch has no Unbounded phrase
  and no Workshop Orange flap. None of the screens above has either.

## Easter eggs

All decorative: hidden from assistive technology, never blocking a control, and still under reduced motion.

| Egg | Where | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Patch on the 404** (`components/feedback/NotFoundPet.tsx`, rules in `lib/patch/pet.ts`) | Any missing page | Patch trots to the pointer (or a tap) and hops, leaves fading paw prints, wanders after 4.5 s alone and naps after 15 s. Click Patch to pat it: eyes closed happily and three orange hearts. Reduced motion: Patch sits still near the right edge. |
| **Hopping loader** (`components/brand/BrandLoader.tsx`) | Dashboard route loading | Three brand dots hop in turn under the walking Patch. |
| **Mood-tile wiggle** (`MoodTile`, `.sw-wiggle` in `app/styles/patch.css`) | Empty states | Hover a mood tile, or a `.group` card around one: the icon gives one quick wiggle. Mouse only. |
| **Layer ticker** (`components/eggs/LayerTicker.tsx`, `layersFor` in `lib/patch/eggs.ts`) | Staff job page of a completed or collected job | Patch (idle pose) watches the print build up as twelve layers, one every 160 ms. Reduced motion: the stack is shown complete. |
| **Beep boop** (`components/eggs/BeepBoop.tsx`, `cameBackInService` in `lib/patch/eggs.ts`) | Staff machine and upkeep pages | When a machine you saw in maintenance or offline is back to idle (this tab remembers the last status), a small "beep boop" shows beside its status for 2.4 s. No Patch, so no extra bold moment. |
| **Colour-mode change event** (`components/providers.tsx`) | Everywhere | Switching light or dark dispatches `swm:theme-change` on `window` in the same frame the tokens change, so anything can follow along; the new mode is revealed as a soft circle from the toggle. |
| **The team's note** (`components/about/*`, `app/styles/eggs.css`) | `/about`, linked from the footer | Click the airmail envelope: the Workshop Orange seal cracks and tumbles, the flap swings back on its teal lining, the letter slides out and opens into a note from Shaan Shoukath on why Space Works Machines exists. "Skip to the letter" and reduced motion go straight to the letter. Its Unbounded title and orange seal are that page's one bold moment. |
